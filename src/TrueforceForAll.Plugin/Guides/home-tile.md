The **Trueforce** widget in SimHub's home-screen **Feedback** section.

**Master** switches the Trueforce effects on and off, and the slider sets their overall strength. It is not your wheel's force feedback strength.

**Audio** switches the **Audio rumble** effect on and off, and the slider sets how much of the game's sound you feel through the rim.

The cog opens the plugin's page, where both sit with the rest of their settings. To hide the widget: **Settings** tab, **Widgets & Display**.
