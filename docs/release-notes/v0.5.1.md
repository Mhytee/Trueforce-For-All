# v0.5.1: Improved effect Test buttons, an adjustable rev light start point, and a more helpful self-test

### Test buttons play the real effect

Until now, the Test buttons have been pretty bad and inaccurate. They played a
stand-in that felt little like the effect on track, and your sensitivity and
threshold settings did nothing to it. Today that changes.

Every effect's **Test** button now plays a short recording from a real drive
through the effect itself, using your settings. Move a slider, press Test, and
you feel the change. While a test plays, the button turns into **Stop** and
fills from left to right to show how much is left. One test plays at a time.

### Simple mode on the Telemetry FFB tab

The Simple and Advanced switch is now at the top of the **Telemetry FFB** tab
too, and it always matches the one on the Effects tab. Simple shows each
feature's on and off and its strength, plus Min force, Damping and the force
direction switches. Advanced adds the shaping, timing and threshold controls.
A control you cannot see keeps working exactly as it is.

### Choose where the rev lights start

A new **Lights start at** slider, under **Add rev light support**, sets how far
up the revs the first light comes on. Lower spreads the lights across more of
the revs, like a rev counter. Higher saves them for the last stretch before you
shift. The strip is full at the redline either way.

- **See it in rpm.** A line under the slider shows where the lights fill for
  the car you are driving.
- **One game, its own setting.** Tick **Separate setting for** a game to give
  it its own start. Every other game keeps the main one.
- **Nothing moves until you touch it.** The slider starts at 85 percent, where
  the lights have always started. Cars that bring their own rev light timing,
  such as Assetto Corsa cars with the CSP Bridge, keep it.

### A clearer self-test

- **Click the status pill to check everything.** The pill at the top of the
  panel now runs the self-test. With the plugin off, it opens the Mode list
  instead.
- **Results as a checklist.** Each check gets its own line and icon, and a
  problem the panel can fix gets a button beside it, such as **Reinstall
  USBPcap**.
- **Force feedback is checked too.** Get into a session and turn the wheel or
  drive while the test runs, and it watches for the game's forces for about 6
  seconds.
- **Copy results.** Copies the whole result, ready to paste into a bug report
  or the Discord.

### Bug fixes and improvements

- **The panel uses less CPU while you drive.** The audio meters only update
  while you can see them, and the panel refreshes less often while it is
  minimized.
- **Ticking several car presets no longer freezes the Presets tab.** The
  Delete count and the buttons beside it now keep up as you tick.
- **Shorter logs with no wheel plugged in**, so the start of the log is still
  there when you attach it to a bug report.
