# v0.3.1: A proper DirectInput effects engine, and car-matched rev lights in Assetto Corsa

This update makes our FFB capture far more accurate, brings automatic LED
pattern matching to Assetto Corsa, and fixes some long-standing issues as well
as bugs introduced in v0.3.0.

### The game's DirectInput effects now play

- **What was missing.** Games send their force feedback as a set of different effects: a constant force, plus springs, dampers, friction, inertia and waveforms. The wheel renders them in its own firmware. Until now, our FFB capture only took the constant force value, leaving force feedback incomplete. That is why the same game could feel lighter and less detailed with the plugin on than with it off.
- **What changed.** The plugin now reads them off the USB connection as the game sends them and renders them into the Trueforce stream alongside the game's force, so you get what the wheel's firmware would have produced plus Trueforce on top. Assetto Corsa's stationary damper is back, for one.

### Automatic LED pattern matching comes to Assetto Corsa

- **Your wheel lights up like the car you are driving.** Cars in Assetto Corsa can carry their own shift light data: the revs they light up at, the colours they use, and how fast they flash at the redline. The plugin now reads that from the car itself and puts it on your rim, so the lights fill and change colour at the same revs as the dash in front of you, and flash along with it. Turn on "Match my wheel to the car I'm driving" and it happens by itself.

Needs the TF4ALL CSP Bridge. Assetto Corsa normally drives the wheel's lights itself, so the plugin asks it to step back, and puts that setting back if you turn our rev lights off.

### Bug fixes

- **The wheel no longer pulls to one side when you pause.** SimHub keeps repeating the last telemetry it had, so the plugin held the force from that instant and pulled the wheel over. It now recognises a pause, either because the game says so or because the readings stop changing. First reported in RaceRoom.
- **Your rev lights no longer stick, go dark, or turn up late.** v0.3.0's new bridge let the wheel's screen work in Assetto Corsa for the first time. That turned out to have a consequence nobody saw coming: with the screen switched on, it and the game were both talking to the wheel at once, and the game's lights lost. They could stick where they were, go dark, then jump to the right level a few seconds later. Only one thing writes to them at a time now. Changing pattern mid-session stops flickering for the same reason.
- **No more unplugging your wheel to get force feedback working.** For some users the plugin's FFB capture would not work at all until they unplugged the wheel and plugged it back in. The plugin now detects a wheel that is not responding and tries to recover it on its own, with no replug needed.

### Interface

- **The pattern preview is shorter.** It used to fill the strip, empty it again, and only then settle on the finished pattern. Now it fills once and holds, which shows the direction the lights run in and the colours themselves without the wait.
- **The What's new window is wider, resizable, and easier to read.** Wrapped notes render as paragraphs instead of running together.
