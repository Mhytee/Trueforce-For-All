> [!WARNING]
> **Installing this pre-release enrolls you in the beta channel.** From this version on, beta updates are open to everyone: future pre-releases arrive through the in-app updater with no sign-in or Patreon needed, and you can leave the channel any time in Settings > Updates.
>
> If you are on v0.2.0 or v0.2.1 without a linked Patreon, those older builds still gate the in-app channel: install this one manually and you are on the open channel from here on.

The beta channel opens to everyone, switching back to stable gets a safety net, and the first round of 0.2.1 field reports is fixed.

### Beta updates for everyone

- **The beta channel is no longer a supporter perk.** Anyone can turn on "Get beta (pre-release) updates in-app" in Settings > Updates, and anyone who installs a pre-release is enrolled automatically, so testers are never stranded on a stale beta waiting for a fix that already shipped. Pre-releases stay public on GitHub as always.
- **Switching back to stable is a real path now.** Turn the beta channel off on a beta build and the updater offers the newest stable release as the way back.

### Your pre-beta setup is backed up

- **Taking your first beta update from a stable install now backs up your presets and settings first.** If you later switch back to stable, the updater offers to restore that backup, so you land exactly where you left off. Anything the restore replaces is parked in an undo folder next to the backup rather than deleted.

### Bug fixes

- **G923 force feedback pass-through could die silently mid-session.** On wheels that share a USB capture with other devices (the G923 composite among them), a mid-session reconnect could leave the FFB tap watching the wrong device's traffic, and pass-through stayed dead until restart. The liveness watchdog now watches only your wheel and recovers as designed.
- **Two simultaneous saves could corrupt a preset file.** Saves of the same file no longer share a temp file, and leftover temps from crashes are cleaned up automatically.
- **Assetto Corsa mod cars with inconsistent folder-name casing split into duplicates.** Car ids now match case-insensitively, so the same car packaged with different casing keeps one row and its saved defaults.
- **Release notes now render properly in-app.** Warning callouts like the one at the top of this page, and links inside the notes, used to show as raw markdown in the update and What's-new windows; they now render like they do on GitHub.
- **Fixed a crash risk in the community preset updates window** when it opened with no updates listed.
