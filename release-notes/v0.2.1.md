Axle slip gets a full set of tuning sliders, all three of the v0.2.0 texture effects now ship enabled with a G PRO tuned baseline, and the first bugs the community caught since launch are fixed.

### Axle slip is now fully tunable

Six new sliders on the Axle slip card:

- **Front strength / Rear strength.** How loud each end of the car speaks, under the main gain.
- **Front pitch / Rear pitch.** Where each voice sits: lower reads as a heavier rub or thud, higher as a sharper hiss. The climb-toward-the-limit character is preserved as you retune.
- **Understeer judder.** How strongly the wheel shudders as the front tires grab and let go past the limit. Zero leaves a smooth scrub with no chatter.
- **Onset threshold.** How close to the grip limit before the voices start speaking, the single biggest lever for how early the warning arrives.

The "Predictive onset" toggle is now "Predictive early warning", with clearer wording about what it predicts.

### The new effects are now on by default

Axle slip, Kerb thump, and Lockup judder now ship enabled, with strengths and pitches tuned on a G PRO as the new baseline. They only speak where the game's telemetry supports them, and choices you have already made are kept: if you turned one off or retuned it, your settings win.

### Community and sharing

- **Join Discord buttons actually link you.** Community messages pointing at the Discord now run the in-app link flow: verifies your account, joins the server, unlocks your roles. Previously they opened a bare invite.
- **No more duplicate presets.** Sharing something identical to a preset already in the community is caught up front.

### For tinkerers

- **RESETGRIP access code.** Wipes the per-car grip auto-calibration for the car you are driving so it re-learns from scratch. Handy after a tune or tire change that leaves the old calibration feeling off; it re-learns on its own within a few minutes of cornering, RESETGRIP just skips the wait.

### Bug fixes

- **Doubled haptics in native Trueforce games while the plugin was disabled:** on a cold start with the plugin toggled off, the plugin kept streaming to the wheel and collided with the game's own Trueforce until you toggled it on and off once. The wheel is now fully released whenever the plugin is disabled, however it got disabled. Thanks to nico-mnd for the report and the logs that nailed it (#37).
- **Axle slip caused violent oscillations under braking:** a locked tire read as wheelspin, slamming the slip voices on at full strength and setting off violent oscillations under hard braking. Locked tires are now recognized and stay quiet, so braking feel has one owner: the Lockup judder.
- **Community share prompts needed a warm-up lap:** the browser's share flow now works on first open instead of needing a visit to another tab first, and its empty states now say something useful for the scope you are viewing.
